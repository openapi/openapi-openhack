> **Enterprise APIs weren't made for weekend hackers.**
> **We think that's a good reason to play with them.**

Somewhere in your house there's a Raspberry Pi collecting dust.
A homelab. An old laptop that still boots. A €5 VPS. A domain you bought for an idea that never shipped.
An AI coding agent waiting for Saturday night.

Now imagine giving all of that **access to the real world**.

### 🚪 Doors to the real world

AI can write the code to send a certified email.
**It can't provide the certified email infrastructure.**

It can generate a PDF.
**It can't physically deliver a letter somewhere in Italy.**

It can calculate a hash.
**It can't certify that a document existed at a precise moment.**

> **Open source and AI provide the software.**
> **Openapi provides the doors to the real world.**

Certified email · physical mail · SMS · timestamps · digital signatures · company data · vehicles · real estate · cadastral data · and much more.
Built for companies. **Left open for you.**

### 🤔 Can it be done?

- 📮 Can a **Git commit** send a physical letter?
- 🍓 Can a **Raspberry Pi** deal with Italian bureaucracy?
- ⏱️ Can we **prove** this source code existed today?
- 💾 Can a **30-year-old computer** query a modern professional API?
- 🔴 Can a **physical button** send certified email?
- 🤖 Can a **GitHub Action** make something happen in the physical world?

Nobody asked. That's the point.

### 🚫 This is not a hackathon

❌ No 48-hour pressure · ❌ No pitches · ❌ No jury · ❌ No business plan · ❌ No startup

✅ Curiosity over usefulness · ✅ Experiments over products · ✅ Old and strange tech · ✅ Unfinished projects · ✅ **Useless is welcome**

40 lines of Bash? Welcome. A broken prototype with a brilliant idea? Very welcome.

### 🔥 Something is coming

Experiments, open challenges, community showcases, and a few surprises for the people who show up first.
